01 — The match

How a night gets a crew

We are paid by a referral cut from vendors. Hosts use the plan form. Vendors use the partner form. Neither form charges a card.

For hosts

  1. 01

    Tell us

    Event type, date, city or region, guest count, budget range, and which vendors you need.

  2. 02

    We match vetted vendors

    Vetted, for now, means we confirm the vendor offers that service and covers your region before we make an introduction. There is no star score on this site.

  3. 03

    You book

    You choose who to hire. Prices come from the vendor, not from a checkout on this website.

For vendors

  1. 01

    Join the network

    Tell us the business, the category, and where you work. The partner form does not start a subscription.

  2. 02

    Get referrals

    When a host needs your category in your region, we send the introduction. Our cut comes from the vendor side.

  3. 03

    Later, lead software

    A separate early-access waitlist for Brickline. Suggested tiers are $49, $99, and $249 a month. Nothing is billed yet.

Vendor leads, still on a waitlist
