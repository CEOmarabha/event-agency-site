Proposed Starter, Pro, and Desk tiers for vendors who want a daily look at new events. $49, $99, and $249 a month are suggestions. Nothing is billed, and there is no checkout.

What the scan is meant to do

Brickline is Khaled’s lead software. The public project is on GitHub (opens in a new tab). For this agency, the waitlist is a daily scan of public records, event calendars, and social media for new events in Chicagoland, Milwaukee and southeast Wisconsin, and northwest Indiana.

Public records

Permits, filings, and other public notices that point at a gathering still being planned.

Event calendars

Published calendars for festivals, park events, halls, and community nights.

Social media

Public posts that announce a wedding, quinceañera, block party, or festival date.

Proposed, not live. This is not a vendor login. Joining the waitlist does not start billing, does not reserve a price, and does not promise a lead volume.
